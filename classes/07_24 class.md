## **60 MIN MAT PILATES-** WARM UP, CORE, ARMS, GLUTES, LOWER BODY, & COOL DOWN

---

**Class title:** Mat Pilates **Duration:** 60 min; **Equipment:** mat 

**Class Purpose and Objectives:** Focus on the mind and body connection, stretch the total body, and work the upper body, core, and legs

**INTRODUCTION (2 Minute)** 

## **Warm-Up (5 Minutes)**

| Duration | Move | Transition cue  | Movment  |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 min | Child's pose | come to table top, hands aligned with shoulders, hips distance apart with a neutral spine  | Bring big toes together, knees wide apart, crawl the hands towards the top of the mat as you lower your hips towards the ankles and place your forehead on the mat.  Side body strentch→ bring right hand to top right corner and stack left hand on top. Inhale back to center, repeat on left. |
| 30 sec | Cat and cows | Lifting the hips away from the heels, meet at tabletop in all fours. Wrists directly underneath shoulder. Knees directly underneath hips. Keep a neutral spine  | Inhale as you initiate movement through the chest, lifting it towards the ceiling, gaze towards the ceiling, and point the tailbone up towards the sky. Exhale as you round the shoulder blades and tilt the pelvis inwards towards the chest, lower the gaze. Making sure were  not dumping lower back |
| 30 sec | Hovering table top→downwards facing dog alternating  | Still in all 4s, pelvis parallel to the ground, palms grounded to mat, lift knees off the mat so tour in a hovering table-top | From hover, inhale lift hips into Downward Dog. Exhale shift forward, shoulders over wrists, lower knees to hover. Repeat. Move with control, core tight, shoulders strong. |
| 1 min 30 sec | Thread the needle (R) Wrist Roatation | Lower knees to mat, Still in all 4s, pelvis parallel to the ground, palms grounded to mat | Inhale, lift right arm up towards the ceiling, gaze over fingertips. end→ sit back onto heels, meet me in a seated position

**Circuit \#1: Core Circuit- 6 min (10 sec rest between)**

| Duration | Move | Transition cue 10 sec | Movment  |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 40 sec | Single leg stretch \-\>Alternating sides | From seated position, grab back of thighs and lower down slowly with control on to mat, extend the legs out | Lift head and shoulders into an abdominal curl with legs in tabletop. Exhale as one leg extends long and the opposite knee draws toward the chest. Inhale as you switch legs in a controlled alternating motion while maintaining abdominal engagement. Inhale for 2, Exhale for 2 R→ keep legs on mat P→ lift chest and legs of mat  |
| 40 sec | Criss-Cross | Same position as before, were adding a twist, alternating the legs in a cycling motion.   | Extend one leg long, and drive oppistive knee in towards the chest as you crunch up and twist with the opposite elbow towards the knee, switch legs. Inhale for 2, Exhale for 2\. Maintain a steady torso and controlled breathing rhythm. P→ continue single leg stretch  |
| 40 sec | Crunch  | Knees bent, feet grounded on the mat.  | Exhale as you lift the head and shoulders, drawing the navel toward the spine and curling the ribcage toward the pelvis. Inhale as you slowly lower back to the mat with control. P→ option to lift legs off the mat at 90 degree angle   |
| 40 sec | Leg circle (R) →reverse direction after 20 sec | Left leg anchored to the mat, lift the right leg towards the ceiling perpendicular the body | Inhale to begin the circle across the body and down, exhale as the leg completes the circle back to center. Maintain a stable pelvis and engaged core. R→Bend the working leg slightly. P→  Perform larger circles   |
| 40 sec | Leg circle (L) →reverse direction after 20 sec  | Right leg anchored to the mat, lift the left leg towards the ceiling perpendicular the body |  Inhale to begin the circle across the body and down, exhale as the leg completes the circle back to center. Maintain a stable pelvis and engaged core. R →Bend the working leg slightly. P→Perform larger circles   |
| 40 sec  | Roll ups | On the mat, inhale to prepare as you stretch the arms above the ears and legs straight out.  | Exhale, lift arms, chest, and shoulder blades as you curl up to a seated position, and reach towards the toes at the top. Inhale, and exhale, scoop the abs back into a C-curve while gently pressing into the ring. Inhale to sit tall. Repeat with control. R→Bend knees with feet anchored on mat.  |

End→come to seated \+ grab water

**Circuit \#2: Glutes \-\> 6 min 20 sec (no break)**

| 40 sec | Full Range Glute Bridge \+ Knee drive (L) | Hold bridge, Maintain lifted hips  | Exhale, lift the ;eft bended knee up as you squeeze the right glute, lower leg, and repeat R→Continue with glute bridge p→ Squeeze at top for longer |
| 40 sec | Static Glute Bridge \+ Knee drive+ pulses (L) | Hold bridge, Maintain lifted left leg | Pulse the lifted leg at the top without lowering hips down  |

**Circuit \#3: Core, glutes and back  \-\> 6 min (10 sec break)**

| Duration | Move | Transition cue (10 sec) | Movment   |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 min | Transition | Role on to stomach with extended legs and arms by sides |  |
| 40 sec | Superman  | Lie prone, arms and legs extended | Exhale, lift arms, chest, and legs off the floor, keep neck neutral, hold, and lower. Repeat  R→ take longer rest at bottom/ dont lift legs P → Lift higher \+ hold longer  |
| 40 sec | Supermen hold \+ flatter arms  | Hold lifted position  | Small alternating arm lifts (flutter), keep chest and legs elevated R→ continue with regular superman P → Lift higher |
| 40 sec | Bird-dog extension with crunch (R)  | Take child's pose, lift hips and come to tabletop on all fours with neutral spine | Tabletop position (hands under shoulders, knees under hips) Extend opposite arm \+ leg, then exhale crunch elbow to knee under body, repeat P→ Slow tempo / hold extension before crunch |
| 40 sec | Bird-dog extension with crunch (L)  | Stay in high tabletop and engage core | Tabletop position (hands under shoulders, knees under hips) Extend opposite arm \+ leg, then exhale crunch elbow to knee under body, repeat P→ Slow tempo / hold extension before crunch |
| 40 sec | Knee push-ups  | Still in tabletop with knees down, shift  forward so body weight is supported by arms  | Knees down, body in straight line. Exhale, lower chest toward floor, press back up p → Full push-ups / slower eccentric |
| 40 sec | High Plank alternating crunch  | Lift knees off the mat coming into a high-plank | Exhale, drive knee toward chest (or elbow), alternate sides R→ Slow march or hold plank P→ Hold at crushed position longer |
| 40 sec | High plank hold | Still in a high plank, hands under shoulders, core tight | Hold strong plank, neutral spine, steady breathing R→ bear hold/ drop knees |

End→Lower down onto knee, sit back for rest+ water and eventually meet me standing

**Circuit \#4- lower body \-\>  7 min 20 sec ( \*no break until switching sides)** 

| Duration | Move | Transition Cue | Movement |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 min rest  | Rest | Cue full circuit (Starting on right side) |  |
| 30 sec | Squat to Stand | Feet hip-width | Sit hips back, exhale stand and squeeze glutes R→Reduce depth/ speed P→ Increase depth |
| 30 sec | Squat Pulse | Stay low | Small pulses, weight in heels R→ continue w regular squats P→ increase depth  |
| 30 sec | Reverse Lunge (R) | Step right leg back | Drop into lunge, push through left heel to stand R→smaller range |
| 30 sec | Reverse Lunge Pulse (R) | Stay in lunge | Small pulses down, chest lifted R→ Continue with normal lunge or smaller range |
| 30 sec | Standing Kickback (R) | Shift weight to left | Extend right leg back, squeeze glute, return slow R→ Bend knee slightly, smaller range P→ hold at top |
| 30 sec | Kickback Hold \+ Pulse (R) | Hold leg back | Small pulses up, keep hips square R→ Bend knee slightly, smaller range |

→ 20 sec break → Repeat on left side

**Circuit \#5: side body  \-\> 11 min 40 sec (\*no break until switching sides)**
